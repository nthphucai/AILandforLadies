**Title**: "Explainable AI for Enhanced Clinical Decision Support in Oncology: A Hybrid Approach Combining Deep Learning and Symbolic Reasoning"

**authors**: ["Dr. Anya Sharma", "Prof. Ben Carter", "Dr. Chen Li", "Maria Rodriguez, MSc"]

**Abstract**: This paper proposes a novel hybrid AI framework designed to improve clinical decision-making in oncology by integrating the predictive power of deep learning with the interpretability of symbolic reasoning. While deep learning models have shown remarkable accuracy in cancer diagnosis and prognosis from complex medical imaging and genomic data, their "black-box" nature often hinders trust and adoption by clinicians. Our framework addresses this challenge by using deep learning for initial pattern recognition and feature extraction, which then feeds into a symbolic knowledge base. This knowledge base, populated with medical guidelines and expert rules, generates human-understandable explanations for the AI's recommendations. Through a retrospective analysis of lung cancer patient data, we demonstrate that this hybrid approach achieves comparable diagnostic accuracy to state-of-the-art deep learning models while significantly enhancing the transparency and explainability of the decision-making process, leading to increased clinician confidence and potentially better patient outcomes.
